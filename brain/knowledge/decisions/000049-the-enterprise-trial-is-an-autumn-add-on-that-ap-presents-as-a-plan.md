# The Enterprise Trial is an Autumn add-on that AP presents as a plan

## Decision

The self-serve 7-day Enterprise Trial is a $0 Autumn **add-on subscription** (`enterprise_trial`) attached next to the customer's current plan, with the trial set per attach (7 days, no card). It carries every Enterprise feature flag plus unlimited seats and team projects, and no `apCredits` item. AP names the platform's plan "Enterprise Trial" while the add-on is live, so the billing page shows the trial, not Free or Team. When the trial ends, users added during it beyond the base plan's seats are deactivated after a 7-day grace period (see Consequences); team projects are deferred. It is offered on Cloud and on self-hosted Enterprise edition, only to platforms on Free, Free legacy, Plus or Team, annual billing included (decided 2026-10-21); every other plan, such as AppSumo, Plus chat, Enterprise or a custom deal, never sees it, and the console refuses it too. Never on Community, which has no billing code. AP Cloud requests Cloud trials with the console's shared secret (`CONSOLE_API_SECRET_KEY`), which only AP Cloud holds; a self-hosted instance uses its own Autumn customer key and can only request `ee` trials. A trial is granted at most once per (platform owner email, edition), enforced by a console trial ledger with a unique constraint on that pair, so one person can trial once on Cloud and once on self-hosted. The ledger stores the platform owner's email, never the email of the admin who clicked, so other admins of the same platform are also refused; platform ownership cannot be transferred, so this also caps each platform at one trial per edition. It starts only after a confirm screen that states what the trial includes, that credits stay the same, the end date, and what happens when it ends. Only platform admins are offered it; non-admins keep today's locked-feature UI with no trial mention. The console sends the drip (a new track in its daily trial job, keyed on the trial ledger): day 0 welcome, day 2 and day 4 feature spotlights, day 6 "ends tomorrow", day 7 "trial ended". The ended email and screen use the existing "Talk to sales" link for the extra 14 days, unchanged (it still rejects free-mail addresses). In the app every member sees an "Enterprise Trial, N days left" pill; admins get a warning banner from one day left saying when Enterprise features switch off, then a one-time "trial ended" screen.

## Context

Clicking any locked feature should start a no-card 7-day trial that grants Enterprise features but no extra credits. A base-plan subscription would replace Free or Team: it evicts Free's credits and `billingEnforced`, replaces and refunds a paying Team subscription with no revert, and the console's `attachTrial` cancels AppSumo lifetime deals.

## Why

An add-on leaves the base plan untouched, so "no extra credits" holds by construction and AP already unions add-on flags (decision 000030). The product wants the trial to read as its own plan, so AP overrides the plan name while the add-on is live instead of making Autumn swap plans.

## Consequences

- Sandbox-verified 2026-09-27: attaching the add-on costs $0, leaves the base plan and its credits untouched (Free 100/day, Team 50,000), and the add-on subscription carries its own `trial_ends_at`. The console must attach with `new_billing_subscription: true`: the default preview for a Team customer moved Team's next $200 invoice to the trial end date, while a separate subscription kept Team's renewal date unchanged. Whether the trial expires on its own at `trial_ends_at` is still unverified.
- `showPoweredBy` comes from the base plan (Free and Team both grant it), and an add-on can only add flags, never remove them, so "Powered by" (builder badge, form footer, community links) stays visible during the trial. This is accepted; AP does not override it.
- A plan with Embedding hides the Activepieces AI provider, except while a self-serve trial runs: then it shows, even if the customer's own deal also includes Embedding. AP checks for the trial when it decides, from the trial cache or Autumn, and counts an ended trial until the plan is refreshed after its end; it stores nothing on the plan (decided 2026-10-22, replacing a plan column; an Autumn switch per plan was rejected earlier because every embed plan would need it set by hand). The Embedding page tells trialers this pairing lasts only for the trial.
- Buying Plus or Team mid-trial does not end the trial: the add-on keeps running to its end date next to the new plan.

- The platform's plan stays Free or Team during the trial; only the screens say "Enterprise Trial". So checks that treat "not free" as "paying" (run concurrency in `rate-limiter-interceptor.ts`, license-key provisioning, platform delete) keep seeing the base plan, and a trial never gets paid-plan limits.
- `trialEndsAt`, `trial.started` and the console's trial helpers read base subscriptions only, so the add-on needs its own reads.
- Anything the trial enabled must stop when it ends; this is tracked as an open launch check.
- Seats after the trial (decided 2026-10-19, replacing the 2026-10-07 rule of a blocking dialog and no automatic removal): when a trial ends with more used seats than the base plan allows, the platform gets a 7-day grace period, shown in the sidebar where the trial countdown was. Every member sees there that the trial ended and that users added during it beyond the plan's seats lose access on that date unless an admin upgrades. Admins also see it in the one-time trial-ended dialog, and their Manage seats button opens the "Deactivate users" dialog with the users who would be removed already ticked and labelled with the date, so they can swap them or confirm. When the grace ends, the users added during the trial who fall outside the plan's capacity are deactivated automatically, the most recently joined first whatever their role; users from before the trial and the owner are never removed, nothing is deleted, and a `trial.seats.deactivated` event reports how many went. Nothing about the grace is stored (decided 2026-10-21): its deadline is the first daily run at or after the trial end plus 7 days, worked out from the trial's dates, and it shows only while the platform is over its seats. A daily job (`trial-seat-grace-sweep`, 15:30 UTC, scheduled next to the license-key usage report) looks only at platforms with more active users and seat-holding invites than their plan allows, checks which of them had the trial, and acts on a deadline from that day back to 3 days late, so a later change to the plan's seats never deactivates trial users unannounced. It re-reads the plan from Autumn before deactivating anyone, and users leave on the date they were shown. A removed user sees "User has been deactivated" on every sign-in path. Embedded users are left alone, do not count toward the grace and are hidden from that Manage seats dialog (2026-10-19), because embedding keeps working after the trial. No email is sent about the deadline. (Free includes 5 users as of 2026-10-19.)
- The seat dialog lists only invites that hold a seat (decided 2026-10-14): pending or accepted platform and project invites from the last 7 days whose email is not yet a user, one row per email. They come from `GET /v1/user-invitations/reserving-seats`, which reuses the seat count's own query, so the list and the count agree. A trial that ends on Free turns project roles off, so platform admins may delete a project invite without that flag (2026-10-15). Before that, one pending project invite kept an admin in the dialog until it expired or they deactivated real users.
- Deferred (2026-09-28, revisit before launch): team projects added during the trial keep working after it ends. The proposed rule is that team projects over the plan's limit are locked through `project_plan.locked`. Nobody can open a locked project or use its API, its flows are disabled, and the UI shows it as locked with a tooltip. Admins can still delete it, and nothing is deleted.
- When a trial ends, AP keeps the Enterprise features on until its cached plan next refreshes, up to 15 minutes. Accepted as is (2026-09-28).
- Once `trial_ends_at` passes, AP treats the add-on as ended even while Autumn still lists it as active: it grants no flags, seats or team projects from it. A late console sweep cannot stretch the trial.
- The console decides who may start a trial and how often; its checks are documented in the console repo (ADR 0013).
- AP stores no trial dates (decided 2026-10-21). Autumn drops the add-on and its dates once the console ends the trial, so the console also keeps the trial's start and end on the Autumn customer, and AP reads them from the customer at each entitlements refresh into a Redis cache (`platform_plan:self-serve-trial:v2:<platformId>`, 24 hours). A refresh that finds no trial never clears a cached one, so a lagging Autumn read right after a start cannot hide it. AP keeps no trial fact in its database at all. An owner who already used the trial on another platform is offered it again there; the console refuses the start, the dialog says the trial was already used, and nothing is remembered, so the offer returns on the next visit (decided 2026-10-21, to keep no second record of what the console knows). Members get the dates as `selfServeTrial` on the platform response.
- In code the feature is the self-serve trial (`selfServeTrial`, `/v1/platform-billing/self-serve-trial`); the Autumn add-on keeps its id `enterprise_trial`, and screens and emails still say Enterprise trial.
- The trial-ended dialog's "talk to sales for 14 more days" button is the same link and behaviour as "Talk to sales" in the Explore plans dialog; there is no self-serve extension.
