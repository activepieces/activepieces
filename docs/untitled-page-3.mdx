---
title: "Usage"
---

The **Usage** page gives platform admins a central place to understand how resources are being consumed across the platform.

At the top of the page, you can see your overall usage for:

- **Credits:** The number of credits used and the credit limit included in your plan.
- **Users:** The number of users on the platform and the user limit included in your plan.

If your plan does not have a limit for a resource, it is shown as **Unlimited**.

### **Credits Usage by Project**

The Project section breaks down credit usage across individual projects, making it easier to see where usage is coming from.

For each project, you can see:

- **AI Usage:** Credits used by AI-related activity.
- **Runs Usage:** Credits used by flow runs.
- **Total:** The total credits used by the project.

If credit usage increases, you can identify which projects are responsible for most of that usage.

You can change the time period to review usage over a specific date range.

<br />

To understand the credit usage break down, checkout the Billing and Subscription page on the platform admin page
