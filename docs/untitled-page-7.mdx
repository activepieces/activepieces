The Triggers page helps platform admins monitor the health and performance of triggers across the platform.

Use this page to identify integrations whose triggers may be failing or behaving unexpectedly, especially when the problem affects multiple flows or projects.

## **Monitor trigger health**

Trigger health is grouped by piece, allowing you to see whether problems are concentrated around a particular integration.

You can review:

- The number of trigger runs over the last 14 days.
- Recent trigger results.
- Trigger health over the last 24 hours, 7 days, and 14 days.
