Go to **Platform Admin → Account → Billing & subscription** to see and manage your subscription.

- **Current subscription**: your plan, and when it renews, switches plan or ends.
- **Seats**: how many users your plan includes and how many are in use.
- **Credits**: AI credits left this period. You can turn on auto recharge so credits top up before they run out.
- **Explore plans**: compare plans and upgrade.
- **Manage subscription**: opens Stripe, where you change your payment method, see invoices or cancel.
- **Refresh**: reloads billing information after a change made in Stripe.
- **License key**: on a self-hosted platform, or with a trial key from our team, paste the key here to activate it.

For how much each project uses, see [Usage](/admin-guide/guides/usage) and [AI usage](/admin-guide/guides/ai-usage).

Subscription changes such as upgrading, cancelling or buying credits always happen on this page by a platform admin. AI assistants connected over MCP can read billing information but cannot change your subscription.
