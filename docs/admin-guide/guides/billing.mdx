The **Billing & subscription** page shows your plan and lets platform admins change it.

Go to **Platform Admin → Account → Billing & subscription**.

### **Current subscription**

- **Plan**: Your current plan, and when it renews, changes or ends.
- **Seats**: How many users your plan includes.
- **Credits**: The AI credits left in this billing period.

### **Change your plan**

**Step 1**: Click **Explore plans**.

**Step 2**: Pick a plan and confirm it.

To cancel, click **Cancel subscription**. You keep your flows, connections and data.

### **Manage payment**

Click **Manage subscription** to open Stripe, where you change your payment method and download invoices. After a change in Stripe, click **Refresh** to load it here.

### **Auto recharge**

Turn on **auto recharge** to buy more AI credits automatically before they run out.

### **License and trial keys**

On a self-hosted platform, or with a trial key from our team, enter the key under **License key** and click **Activate**.

For how much each project uses, see [Usage](/admin-guide/guides/usage) and [AI usage](/admin-guide/guides/ai-usage).

AI assistants connected over [MCP](/admin-guide/guides/mcp-server) can read billing information, but can't change your plan, payment or credits.
