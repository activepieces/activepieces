## Default Behavior

When users connect to services like Google Sheets or Slack, they see "Activepieces" as the app requesting access. This works out of the box with no setup required.

## Why Replace OAuth2 Apps?

- **Branding**: Show your company name instead of "Activepieces" in authorization screens
- **Higher Limits**: Some services have stricter rate limits for shared OAuth apps
- **Compliance**: Your organization may require using company-owned credentials

## How to Configure

1. In your OAuth provider's developer console, register your instance redirect URI:
   - Standard instances: `https://<your-instance-domain>/redirect`
   - If you use an **Embed Subdomain**, register `https://<your-embed-subdomain>/redirect` alongside `https://<your-instance-domain>/redirect` before enabling the subdomain so existing platform OAuth apps do not fail with `redirect_uri_mismatch`.
2. Go to **Platform Admin → Catalogue → Pieces**
3. Find the piece you want to configure (e.g., Google Sheets)
4. Click the lock icon to open the OAuth2 settings
5. Enter your own Client ID and Client Secret
