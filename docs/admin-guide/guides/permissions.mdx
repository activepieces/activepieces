Activepieces utilizes Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for managing permissions within projects. Each project consists of multiple flows and users, with each user assigned specific roles that define their actions within the project.

The Users tab is a dedicated place for your platform admin to manage, delete, activate, and deactivate users at the entire platform level, not just from projects. 

### How To Invite a User To Your Platform

**Step 1**: Click on Users in the platform admin

**Step 2**: Navigate to the upper right and click the **invite** button

**Step 3**: Add the invitee’s email and assign them a role 

**Step 4:** Click **Invite**

### **How To Edit User Role**

**Step 1**: Go to Users in the platform admin

**Step 2**: Click on the 3 dots to the right of the user you want to edit

**Step 3**: Click on Edit

<video src="https://cdn.activepieces.com/videos/docs/Edit-user-role.mp4" controls />

#### <br /><br />Default Roles

Activepieces comes with three project roles out of the box. The table below shows what each role can view (V) and edit (E) in a project:

| Permission | Admin | Editor | Viewer |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Flows | V, E | V, E | V |
| Flow status (turn a flow on or off) | E | E |  |
| Folders, tables, agents, knowledge base, variables, MCP servers | V, E | V, E | V |
| Runs (view, retry) | V, E | V, E | V |
| Alerts | V, E |  |  |
| App connections | V, E | V, E | V |
| Replace connections | E | E |  |
| Project releases and Git Sync | V, E | V, E |  |
| Project members | V, E | V | V |
| Invitations | V, E | V | V |
| Project settings | V, E | V | V |

#### Custom Roles

If the default roles don't fit your needs, you can create custom roles with specific permissions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roles & Access">
    In the platform admin, go to **Users** → **Roles & Access**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Role">
    Click **New role** and give it a name. The name must differ from every other role, including the default ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Permissions">
    Pick a starting point (**Nothing**, **Viewer** or **Editor**), tick or untick the permissions you want, and click **Create role**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the Role to a Member">
    Open the project, open its members, and pick the new role in the member's role list. The member gets the permissions of the role at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Custom roles are useful when you need fine-grained control, such as allowing users to view and retry runs without being able to edit flows.
</Tip>

### **Deactivate & Delete User:**

**Step 1**: Click on Users in the platform admin

**Step 2**: Click on the 3 dots to the right of the user you want to delete or deactivate

**Step 3:** Click on either Delete or Deactivate depending on what you want 

![Delete Or Deactivate User](https://cdn.activepieces.com/assets/delete-or-deactivate-user.png "Delete Or Deactivate User")

**_NB_**: 

- If you **Deactivate a user**, they will lose access but won't lose all their data. They cannot sign in, and a session they already have open stops working. You can activate them again when your plan has a free seat.
- However, if you **Delete** a user, all their data will be deleted as well.

### **Managing users in larger organizations**

For larger teams, managing users one by one can quickly become difficult. Instead, you can use [**Single Sign-On (SSO)**](/admin-guide/guides/sso) to let users sign in with your organization’s identity provider and [**SCIM**](/admin-guide/guides/scim/overview) to automate user provisioning and deprovisioning.

This makes it easier to manage access as your organization grows without manually inviting, activating, or deactivating every user.
