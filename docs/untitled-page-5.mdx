The **Workers** page gives platform admins a central place to view the workers running your Activepieces flows and monitor their health and resource usage. A worker is responsible for executing flows. 

The worker page on your platform admin is designed for monitoring your existing workers.

For information about configuring and operating workers, including worker sizing, scaling, concurrency, failure recovery, sandboxing, and network isolation, see the[ **<u>Workers documentation</u>**](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/install/architecture/workers).

For a deeper explanation of how workers operate, scale, and recover from failures, see the[ **<u>Workers documentation</u>**](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/install/architecture/workers).

## **Monitor worker health**

Each worker is displayed separately so you can see its current status and resource usage.

For each worker, you can view:

- **Status:** Whether the worker is currently online.
- **CPU:** How much processing capacity the worker is currently using.
- **RAM:** How much memory the worker is using.
- **Disk:** How much disk space is being used.
- **Last seen:** How recently Activepieces received a health update from the worker.

You can use this information to quickly check whether your workers are online and identify workers experiencing unusually high resource usage.
