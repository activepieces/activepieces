  topped up on any paid tier via add-on packs.

from purchase date. After 12 months, unused paid credits expire.

# PART 6 — WHAT COUNTS

## How we measure usage

**An execution** is one complete run of a workflow, from trigger to finish.
A workflow with 10 steps counts as 1 execution, not 10.

**A step** is one action or trigger inside a workflow. Every workflow has at
least one trigger and one action. The step allowance is the maximum number
of steps a single workflow can contain.

**An active workflow** is a workflow whose status is ENABLED. Drafts and
disabled workflows don't count against the active workflow limit.

**An AI credit** is 1,000 tokens of AI processing (input + output). A short
chat message might use 2-3 credits. A long prompt with a long response might
use 15-20. Image generation is 20 credits per image. Audio transcription is
10 credits per minute.

**A workspace** is an isolated environment with its own workflows, agents,
tables, connections, credentials, usage, and permissions. Data in one
workspace is never visible to another.

**A seat** is one user with access to a workspace. Owners, administrators,
and members all count as one seat each.

**An email** is one outbound transactional email sent by Synkra Flow on
your behalf.

**An SMS** is one outbound SMS message.

**A WhatsApp message** is one outbound WhatsApp message.

**A voice minute** is 60 seconds of outbound or inbound call time.

# PART 7 — DISCOUNTS

## Student and Community Access

Two discount programmes are available on top of the standard plans. Both
give you the exact same plan, limits, and features at a lower price.

**Student pricing** is 35% off any paid plan. Available to anyone currently
enrolled at a higher education institution. Verification is done through
your institution email or a supporting document.

**Community Access** is 60% off any paid plan. Available to qualifying
non-profits, NGOs, community organisations, and faith-based organisations
running a genuine community initiative. Applications are reviewed by a
person on our team.

Only one discount applies per account. If you qualify for both, you receive
the larger discount. Discounts do not stack.

Discounts apply to the workspace they are granted on. Each workspace must
independently qualify.

# PART 8 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Is the Free plan limited in features or only in volume?

Both. The Free plan includes every core feature but with lower volume
limits. Some advanced features (SSO, API keys, audit logs, custom domains)
are Business-only. White label and BYOK are Pro-only.

### What is the difference between Starter and Business?

Business includes more seats (10 vs 3), more workspaces (3 vs 1), higher
execution limits (50,000 vs 15,000), higher AI credits (5,000 vs 1,000),
higher storage (50 GB vs 10 GB), and unlocks SSO, API keys, custom domain,
and audit logs.

### What is the difference between Business and Pro?

Pro includes more seats (25 vs 10), more workspaces (10 vs 3), higher
execution limits (150,000 vs 50,000), higher AI credits (20,000 vs 5,000),
higher storage (200 GB vs 50 GB), and unlocks white label and BYOK.

### Can I start on Free and upgrade later?

Yes. Start on Free, upgrade at any time. Your data, workflows, and
configurations carry over. Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged
at the full new plan price.

### Can I downgrade from a paid plan to Free?

Yes. Downgrade schedules for the end of your current billing period. You
keep your current plan until then. On the next renewal, you move to Free.

### Do add-on packs count toward my plan's monthly allowance?

No. Add-on packs sit alongside your plan allowance. Your plan allowance is
consumed first. Add-on packs are drawn down only after your plan allowance
is used up.

### Do add-on packs expire?

Paid add-on credits roll over for up to 12 months from purchase date. After
12 months, unused paid credits expire. Included plan credits reset monthly
and do not roll over.

### What happens if I run out of AI credits?

AI features pause. Chat, agents, and AI steps in workflows are blocked
until you buy more credits or upgrade your plan. Non-AI features continue
to work. You receive an in-app notification and an email.

### What happens if I run out of executions?

Production flow runs are blocked. You can still build, edit, and test
workflows. The QUOTA_EXCEEDED error appears in execution history. Buy an
upgrade or wait for the monthly reset.

### Can I use the Free plan for a real business?

Yes, within the limits. Free includes 500 executions per month, 5 active
workflows, and 10 steps per workflow. If you exceed those limits, upgrade
to Starter.

### Is there a contract?

No. Monthly subscriptions, cancel anytime. No minimum commitment. Annual
plans are available on request.

### What payment methods do you accept?

Card payments via Paystack. Instant EFT and debit orders available on
request for annual plans.

### Where is my data stored?

Workspaces are hosted on infrastructure in Germany. Primary database,
file storage, and backups are in South Africa. See the Privacy Policy for
full details.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Workflows can be exported as JSON. Tables as CSV. Execution history
as JSON. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

### What is BYOK and why is it Pro-only?

BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) lets you use your own OpenAI, Claude, or other
AI provider credentials instead of Synkra's AI. It's Pro-only because it
requires additional security handling and is designed for customers with
existing AI provider relationships.

### Do you offer an Agency Programme?

Yes. Agencies that manage multiple clients can join the Agency Programme
with wholesale rates and client environment management. See the Agency
Programme documentation for details.

### How do I get support?

Free: community forum and best-effort email.
Starter: email support, 48 business hours target.
Business: priority email, 8 business hours target.
Pro: priority email + WhatsApp, 2 business hours target.

Business hours: Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 SAST.
